Unique Tasty drinks are best within 3 to 4 weeks and should be kept chilled. That is the short answer. Here is the longer one.

## Put them in the fridge first

Put your bottles in the fridge as soon as they arrive. We keep them chilled until they leave us, and the fridge keeps them that way.

## Check the date on the label

Each bottle has a date on the label. Count 3 to 4 weeks from it. If you buy a 12-pack, drink the oldest bottles first.

## Shake before you pour

The ingredients settle. A gentle shake mixes the bottle again, and the drink tastes the way it should.

## Once a bottle is open

Keep it in the fridge with the cap closed and finish it within a day or two. This is the same way you would handle any fresh drink you open at home.

## Do not leave bottles in the heat

A bottle left in a hot car or on a sunny counter will spoil faster. If you pick up on a hot day, bring a cool bag.

## When to stop

If a sealed bottle tastes or smells wrong, do not drink it. Message us on WhatsApp the same day with your order number and a photo and we will make it right. You can read what we do on the returns page.

## Planning an order around shelf life

Buy the pack you can finish in a few weeks. If you are ordering for an office, order every week or every month so the fridge always holds fresh bottles. Our custom order page has the details.

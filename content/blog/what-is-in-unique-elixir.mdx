Below, we go through each ingredient and say what it brings to the glass. We describe taste only.

## Hibiscus

Hibiscus is the dried red flower that gives zobo its colour. In the Elixir it brings a deep red colour and a tart edge. The first sip is where you notice it most.

## Pineapple

Pineapple brings sweetness and brightness. It rounds off the tartness of the hibiscus so the drink does not turn sharp.

## Beetroot

Beetroot gives the drink its earthy base and a little natural sweetness. It is also part of the dark red colour.

## Cucumber

Cucumber keeps the taste clean and cool. It is the quiet ingredient that stops the stronger flavours from crowding each other.

## Lemon

Lemon adds a sharp, fresh note. A small amount lifts the whole drink.

## Ginger

Ginger brings warmth and a little spice that you feel at the back of the mouth.

## Cinnamon

Cinnamon adds a warm, sweet spice that sits under the fruit.

## Cloves

Cloves are strong and spicy. A little goes a long way, and they finish the drink.

## How it comes together

Tart hibiscus and sweet pineapple lead. Beetroot holds the middle. Cucumber and lemon keep it clean. Ginger, cinnamon and cloves finish it. That is the taste in one line.

Serve it cold. Shake the bottle before you pour. Drink it on its own, over ice, or beside a meal.

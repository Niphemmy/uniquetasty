Unique Elixir comes in two sizes: 250ml for <Price sku="ELX-250-1" /> and 500ml for <Price sku="ELX-500-1" />. A 500ml bottle holds twice what a 250ml bottle holds. Choose by how you plan to drink it.

## Choose 250ml when

- You want a drink for one sitting.
- You are putting a bottle in a bag or a lunchbox.
- It is your first time and you want to taste before you commit.
- You want to give one bottle to someone as a small gift.

## Choose 500ml when

- You want a longer drink, or you want to share a bottle across the table.
- You are ordering for two people at home.
- You have a fridge with space and you like to pour a glass at a time.

## A simple way to decide

1. Never tried it? One 250ml single.
2. Liked it and want it at home? A 6-pack of 250ml.
3. Buying for the household or the office fridge? A 12-pack.
4. Sharing at a table? A 500ml bottle.

## Remember the shelf life

Bottles are best within 3 to 4 weeks and should stay chilled. If you live alone, a 12-pack is a lot to finish in that time. Pick the pack you can drink in a few weeks, or share it.

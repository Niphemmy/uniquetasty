Every bottle of Unique Elixir is made in small batches in our kitchen in Old Bodija, Ibadan. This is how a batch goes from ingredients to the fridge.

## Step 1: Buying the ingredients

We buy beetroot, hibiscus, pineapple, cucumber, lemon, ginger, cinnamon and cloves. We look at each one before it comes into the kitchen.

## Step 2: Washing and preparing

Beetroot, ginger, pineapple, cucumber and lemon are washed and cut. Hibiscus is rinsed.

## Step 3: Making the batch

We make each batch in small amounts. We use the same recipe each time, so the drink tastes the same from one batch to the next.

## Step 4: Bottling

Each bottle is filled, closed and checked.

## Step 5: Labelling

We label each bottle. The label lists the ingredients and the date.

## Step 6: Chilling and delivery

Bottles go into the fridge straight away. They stay chilled until they are picked up or delivered.

## Why small batches

A small batch means your bottle has not been sitting for months. The label tells you the date, and the drink is best within 3 to 4 weeks.
